Fleet Solutions

A central element of the passenger experience is the quality of the aircraft cabin environment.

To ensure cabins are clean and comfortable, the EGS fleet solution team provides an around-the-clock cabin preparation service for hundreds of thousands of flights per year across international and local airports.

The Company Provides the Following Aircraft Services:

  • Cabin Grooming and Deep Cleaning.
  • Turnaround Cleaning.
  • Toilet and Water Services.
  • Disinfection Services.
