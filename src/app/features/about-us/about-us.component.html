About Us

The first Ground Services Company established in Egypt, Africa, and the Middle East region.

EGYPTAIR GROUND SERVICES is the first ground handling company in Africa and the Middle East region, and the fifth in the world to provide its services as an ISAGO provider since 2008.

Our services are provided at Cairo (CAI), Sharm El Sheikh (SSH), Hurghada (HRG), Borg El Arab (HBE), Luxor (LXR), and Aswan (ASW) stations.
