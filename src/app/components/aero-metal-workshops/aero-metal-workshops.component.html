Aero Metal Workshops

AeroMetal Provides advanced metal products to cover the needs of the holding company, its subsidiaries and external customers.

Our target is to Upgrade our technologies in order to be able to face the competition and increase our market share.

AERO METAL is important factory in the ground services company, as it contains seven main workshops.

Dollies Workshop

Design and manufacturing for all types of dollies used inside the airport like:

  • Pallet dollies.
  • Double container dollies.
  • Single container dollies.

The workshop also performs all required maintenance processes needed for all types of dollies.

Containers Workshop

Assembly operations for the following container types:

  1. Container AKE model.
  2. Container AKH model.

The workshop also performs all required maintenance processes needed for all types of containers like:

  1. Container AKE model.
  2. Container AKH model.
  3. Pallet PMC model.
  4. Pallet PLA model.

Forging Workshop

Design and Manufacturing for all types of luggage carts used inside the airport area.

Maintenance processes needed for:

  1. Closed Luggage Carts.
  2. Opened Luggage Carts.

The workshop also manufactures aircraft maintenance stairs and different metallic products according to customer needs.

Other Workshops

Machining Workshop

Manufacturing and supplying other workshops with most metal parts used for manufacturing and maintenance operations.

Painting Workshop

Painting all manufactured dollies, luggage carts and other metal products.

Sewing Workshop

Manufacturing and maintenance for all curtains and nets used for containers and baggage carts.
